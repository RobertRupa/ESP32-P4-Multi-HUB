# C6 ESP-Hosted OTA and Target Audit

- Updated: 2026-11-08
- C6 target: integrated ESP32-C6, ESP-Hosted co-processor over SDIO RPC v2.
- P4 host: ESP32-P4 revision 3.2, encrypted-flashed on `/dev/ttyACM0`; C6 OTA is staged in the P4 `c6_fw` partition and transferred over SDIO. No direct C6 flash or C6 NVS erase was used.

## Supported profiles

- **P4+C6:** C6 Wi-Fi plus native Zigbee coordinator/Peer Data. This profile builds. Historical C6 Zigbee-SDIO OTA and reboot verification succeeded, but the latest recorded BDB network-formation attempt remained `IN_PROGRESS` until the 120-second watchdog (`ESP_ERR_TIMEOUT` 263). No device join is claimed.
- **P4+C6+H2:** C6 Wi-Fi-only; Zigbee ownership belongs to the P4/H2 RCP path. The Wi-Fi-only C6 profile builds but has not been installed. C6 firmware/NVS were left untouched when P4 returned to the H2 host profile.

## C6 image status

- A C6 native-Zigbee SDIO image was previously installed through P4-hosted OTA and its OTA transfer was verified after reboot. C6 Zigbee formation did not complete in the recorded test; no device join is claimed.
- The C6 Wi-Fi-only image for P4+C6+H2 builds, but has not been installed. The CP version/OTA audit can be read separately from the active C6 application flavor; do not infer that C6 Zigbee is disabled from ESP-Hosted version 3.1.0 alone.
- During the latest P4 H2-profile changes, C6 firmware and NVS were not written or erased.

The upstream optional TinyUSB network-mode Kconfig emits three warnings when TinyUSB dependencies are skipped. These do not fail either C6 build. C6 target builds and OTA verification do not establish device joins, RF coexistence, Matter Bridge behavior, or Thread functionality.
